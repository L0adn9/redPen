Scenario
The Student Affairs office runs a campus event booking service. Business rules:
1. A student can book many events; an event can have many student bookings, up to the venue capacity.
2. Each event is held in exactly one venue on one date and time slot; a venue hosts many events.
3. Each event is organised by one club; a club organises many events. Each club has one advisor (a staff member).
4. Some events charge a fee; payments are recorded per booking with a payment method and date.
5. Students may join a waiting list when an event is full; the waiting list keeps the order of joining.

Unnormalised booking record (as kept today in a spreadsheet):
BookingID, StudentID, StudentName, StudentEmail, ProgrammeCode, ProgrammeName, EventID, EventTitle, EventDate, Slot, VenueID, VenueName, VenueCapacity, ClubID, ClubName, AdvisorStaffID, AdvisorName, Fee, PaymentMethod, PaymentDate

Tasks
T1 (30 marks) Draw an ERD (crow's foot) for the business rules above. State all assumptions.
T2 (25 marks) Normalise the unnormalised booking record to 3NF, showing 1NF, 2NF and 3NF with the dependencies at each step.
T3 (25 marks) Write SQL to (a) create the tables for Event, Venue and Booking with keys and constraints, (b) list events that are fully booked, (c) show each club's total fee income for 2026.
T4 (20 marks) Reflection (max 500 words): explain two design decisions you changed after the Week 5 tutorial design review, and why.

Submission: one PDF report plus a .sql file, Week 8.
