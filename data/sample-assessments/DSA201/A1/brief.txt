Scenario

Tasks
T1 (20 marks) Explain how bubble sort, merge sort, quick sort and binary search work, including their best, average and worst-case time complexity.
T2 (15 marks) Produce a comparison table of the four algorithms covering time complexity, space complexity and stability.
T3 (30 marks) Recommend which sorting and searching algorithms the library should use for the stock report and for ISBN/title search. Justify your choice for this scenario, with reference to the data size and how often each operation runs.
T4 (25 marks) Implement your chosen search algorithm in Python and show test cases with their outputs.
T5 (10 marks) Presentation, structure and referencing (APA 7th).
